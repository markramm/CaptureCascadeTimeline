New York Attorney General Barbara Underwood filed lawsuit against the Donald J. Trump Foundation and its board directors - Donald Trump, Donald Jr., Ivanka, and Eric Trump - alleging "shocking pattern of illegality" spanning over a decade. The suit documented systematic self-dealing where Trump used the Foundation as his "personal checkbook" to pay legal settlements, purchase personal items including a $10,000 portrait of himself, and promote Trump hotels and businesses with charitable assets. The AG alleged unlawful coordination with the 2016 presidential campaign, including using Foundation funds raised from the public to support campaign activities in violation of federal election law. The lawsuit sought $2.8 million in restitution, Foundation dissolution under court supervision, and 10-year ban on Trump serving as nonprofit director in New York (one year for his children). Underwood sent referral letters to IRS and Federal Election Commission for potential federal violations. The case demonstrates how wealthy individuals exploit nonprofit structures for personal enrichment and political benefit while facing minimal criminal consequences.
