title: DOJ Sues 21 States and DC for Refusing to Turn Over Detailed Voter Data
summary: The Department of Justice sues the District of Columbia and at least 21 states, most controlled by Democrats, after they refuse to comply with federal demands for detailed voter registration data. The unprecedented data collection effort alarms election officials who see it as a pretext for voter intimidation and purging voter rolls. The Trump administration claims the data is needed for election integrity, but critics note the timing and scope suggest preparation for challenging voter eligibility and potentially interfering with future elections. This represents an escalation of federal government efforts to centralize control over state election systems and voter information, raising concerns about mass surveillance of voters and potential suppression efforts.
