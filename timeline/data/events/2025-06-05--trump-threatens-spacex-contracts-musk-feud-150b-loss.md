President Trump publicly threatened to cancel SpaceX's federal contracts during a public dispute with Elon Musk, causing an estimated $150 billion in market capitalization loss for the company. The threat demonstrated Trump's willingness to weaponize government procurement power against even his closest allies when they fall out of favor.

The feud erupted over Musk's criticism of a Trump policy decision, prompting Trump to post on Truth Social suggesting that SpaceX's lucrative NASA and Defense Department contracts could be reconsidered. SpaceX holds billions in federal contracts for satellite launches, astronaut transport, and military communications.

The incident revealed the precarious position of government contractors under an administration that views federal spending as a tool of personal loyalty enforcement. Companies dependent on federal contracts face enormous pressure to avoid any public criticism of Trump or his policies.

Market analysts noted the irony that Musk—who spent $294 million supporting Trump's 2024 campaign and served as head of DOGE—could so quickly become a target of Trump's retaliation. The episode demonstrated that no amount of political support provides lasting protection from Trump's transactional approach to governance.
