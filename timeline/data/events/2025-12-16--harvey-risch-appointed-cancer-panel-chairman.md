HHS announced the appointment of Harvey Risch, M.D., Ph.D., as chairman of the President's Cancer Panel. Risch, a Yale epidemiology professor emeritus, is known for promoting hydroxychloroquine and ivermectin as COVID-19 treatments despite lack of evidence, and for speculating about "turbo cancer" potentially linked to COVID-19 vaccines.

The President's Cancer Panel monitors the National Cancer Institute's National Cancer Program and reports to the president on cancer prevention progress. HHS Secretary RFK Kennedy Jr. stated Risch "brings the expertise and resolve needed to identify the root causes of cancer in America."

The official HHS announcement came 11 days after Risch himself announced the appointment on social media and less than two hours after WIRED magazine noted he wasn't listed on the panel's website. Risch replaces Elizabeth M. Jaffee, a respected oncologist appointed by President Biden in January 2023.

The appointment places a promoter of COVID misinformation in charge of federal cancer policy, raising concerns about evidence-based cancer research and treatment. Critics worry Risch may use the position to promote unproven treatments and discourage vaccination—which prevents several cancers including HPV-related malignancies.
