The Department of Homeland Security issued a press release on Thanksgiving Day highlighting ICE arrests conducted while "American families gathered," framing immigration enforcement as a patriotic contrast to family holidays. The release was part of a broader propaganda campaign to justify mass deportation policies.

The statement emphasized the number of arrests made over the holiday period and characterized ICE agents as sacrificing family time to protect Americans. Critics noted the release seemed designed to dehumanize immigrants by contrasting "American families" with enforcement targets.

The Thanksgiving propaganda represented an escalation of DHS communications strategy under the Trump administration, using holidays as messaging opportunities to reinforce anti-immigrant narratives. Similar releases were issued around other holidays throughout 2025.

Immigration advocates criticized the framing as deliberately divisive, noting that many people targeted by ICE are themselves parents, spouses, and family members whose loved ones were separated during the holiday. The propaganda approach treats enforcement statistics as achievements to be celebrated rather than family separations with human costs.
