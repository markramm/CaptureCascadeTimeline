ICE arrested 19-year-old Babson College student Any Lucia Lopez Belloza at Boston Logan Airport and deported her within 48 hours despite a federal court order explicitly prohibiting her removal. The deportation represents one of the most flagrant instances of the administration defying judicial authority.

A federal judge had issued an order staying Belloza's removal pending review of her case, but ICE proceeded with deportation anyway. The agency claimed it was unaware of the court order, though attorneys said they had notified ICE multiple times through official channels.

Belloza, a business student with no criminal record, was returning from a trip when ICE agents detained her. Her attorneys were given minimal notice and no meaningful opportunity to invoke the court's protection before she was removed from the country.

The case exemplifies the administration's pattern of treating court orders as obstacles to be circumvented rather than legitimate constraints on executive power. Legal observers noted that ICE's claimed ignorance of the court order strains credibility given the documentation attorneys provided, suggesting deliberate defiance of judicial authority.
