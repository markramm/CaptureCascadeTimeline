ICE agent Jonathan Ross shoots and kills Renee Good, a 37-year-old American citizen and mother of three, in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Good was in her SUV during an ICE operation when Ross fired three shots through her window, killing her.

Multiple videos captured the incident:
- Ross circled Good's vehicle on foot
- Other agents approached and pulled her door handle
- Good began driving away from Ross, turning into traffic
- Ross fired as her wheels were turned away from him

Good's last words: "Dude, that's fine, I'm not even mad at you."
Ross's words over her body: "Fucking bitch."

The Trump administration immediately defended the shooting:
- Trump claimed Good "violently, willfully, and viciously ran over" the agent
- DHS Secretary Noem defended the shooting
- VP Vance called Good a "domestic terrorist"
- Officials labeled her an "attempted murderer"

All claims were contradicted by video evidence showing Good turning away from Ross, not toward him.

Good had no criminal record. She was a poet and legal observer at ICE protests with her wife Rebecca. She left behind three children.

This became the first fatal ICE shooting of an American citizen during immigration enforcement operations, triggering massive protests, federal troop surges (3,000+ agents deployed to Minneapolis), state lawsuits, and the DOJ cover-up that led to mass prosecutor resignations.
