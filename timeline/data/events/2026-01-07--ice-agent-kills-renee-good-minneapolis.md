ICE agent Jonathan Ross shot and killed Renee Nicole Macklin Good, a 37-year-old US citizen, mother of three, poet and writer, through her car windshield during the DHS's 'largest immigration operation ever' in Minneapolis. Good, who had just dropped her 6-year-old son at school, was not a target of any law enforcement investigation. Eyewitnesses reported agents gave conflicting orders—one telling her to drive away while another shouted to get out. Ross fired three shots through her windshield in 698 milliseconds, hitting her in the head. Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey called the federal narrative 'bullshit' and 'garbage' after viewing video evidence. DHS Secretary Kristi Noem defended the shooting, calling it 'a domestic act of terrorism' by Good. Vice President JD Vance claimed ICE agents have 'absolute immunity' from state prosecution—a claim constitutional experts called 'absolutely ridiculous.' Governor Tim Walz proclaimed January 9 a 'Day of Unity' in Good's memory. The shooting occurred approximately 260 miles from the Canadian border.
