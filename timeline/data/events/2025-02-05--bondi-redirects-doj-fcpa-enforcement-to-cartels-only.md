title: AG Bondi Issues Memo Narrowing FCPA Enforcement to Cartel-Related Cases Only
summary: On her first day as Attorney General, Pam Bondi issues a directive fundamentally reshaping Foreign Corrupt Practices Act enforcement. The memo instructs the FCPA Unit to prioritize only investigations into foreign bribery schemes that facilitate operations of cartels and transnational criminal organizations, effectively abandoning enforcement against corporate foreign bribery. The directive eliminates the KleptoCapture Task Force and the Kleptocracy Asset Recovery Initiative (KARI), dismantling the infrastructure for combating international corruption and kleptocracy. This policy shift protects corporations engaging in foreign bribery while claiming to target criminal cartels, creating a massive enforcement gap that enables corrupt business practices globally.
