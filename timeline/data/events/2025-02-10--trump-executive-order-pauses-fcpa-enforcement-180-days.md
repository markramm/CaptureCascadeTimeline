title: Trump Executive Order Pauses All FCPA Enforcement for 180 Days
summary: President Trump issues an executive order effectively halting Foreign Corrupt Practices Act enforcement for 180 days, with potential extension for another 180 days. The order directs AG Bondi to halt initiation of new FCPA investigations and enforcement actions and review existing matters with an eye toward "restoring proper bounds" on enforcement. Trump claims pausing enforcement will further American economic and national security, but the order creates a window where corporations can engage in foreign bribery without federal prosecution. The pause does not address SEC civil enforcement, creating jurisdictional confusion. This represents the most significant rollback of anti-corruption enforcement in U.S. history, signaling to corporations that foreign bribery will no longer face serious consequences.
