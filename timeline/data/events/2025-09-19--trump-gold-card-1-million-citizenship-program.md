President Trump signed an executive order establishing the "Trump Gold Card"—a pay-to-play citizenship program allowing foreign nationals to purchase permanent residency and a pathway to citizenship for a $1 million investment. The program bypasses traditional immigration requirements in favor of wealth.

The Gold Card creates a two-tier immigration system: skilled workers face $100,000 H-1B fees while wealthy individuals can purchase citizenship directly. The program has no skills, education, or employment requirements—only the ability to pay.

Critics described the program as institutionalizing kleptocracy by explicitly selling American citizenship to the highest bidders. Unlike existing investor visa programs with job creation requirements, the Gold Card has no economic benefit mandate—just payment to the federal government.

The "Trump" branding of the program raised additional concerns about conflating the presidency with personal brand promotion. Ethics watchdogs noted the program could attract wealthy individuals seeking to escape accountability in their home countries, potentially including oligarchs, corrupt officials, and criminals seeking a safe haven.
