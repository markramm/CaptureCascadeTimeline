On June 12, 2025, the Commerce Department released a Federal Register notice expanding the list of steel derivative products subject to Section 232 tariffs at the 50 percent rate established May 30. The following day, June 13, the Trump administration announced the tariffs would apply to the steel content of eight more consumer product lines effective June 23: dishwashers, refrigerators, washing machines, dryers, freezers, stoves, ovens, and food waste disposals. This expansion came after Commerce established a formal process in May 2025 for adding steel and aluminum derivatives to tariff coverage, significantly broadening the scope beyond industrial products to common household appliances.
