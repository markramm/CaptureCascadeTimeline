Attorney General Pam Bondi ordered the dismissal of all federal charges against Dr. Michael Kirk Moore Jr., a Utah plastic surgeon on trial for COVID-19 vaccine fraud. Moore had been indicted by a federal grand jury in January 2023 for destroying more than $28,000 worth of government-provided vaccines and distributing nearly 2,000 fraudulent vaccination cards in exchange for cash.

The indictment also alleged Moore administered saline shots to children at parents' requests so children would believe they received the vaccine. Moore faced up to 35 years in prison on charges including conspiracy to defraud the United States.

Bondi announced the dismissal on social media, stating "Dr. Moore gave his patients a choice when the federal government refused to do so," thanking Rep. Marjorie Taylor Greene and Sen. Mike Lee for bringing the case to her attention. The case had already passed internal DOJ review for "weaponization" before Bondi's intervention.

The dismissal emboldened at least 11 other healthcare professionals facing similar charges to seek case reexaminations. The action signals that vaccine fraud is no longer a prosecutorial priority and rewards anti-vaccine ideology over public health and the rule of law.
