Following Trump's January 20, 2025 executive orders targeting DEI programs and threatening civil investigations, over 1,000 nonprofits removed or diluted diversity, equity, and inclusion language from their IRS Form 990 filings. ProPublica's analysis of 2025 electronic tax filings found systematic removal of DEI-related keywords from mission statements.

Organizations scrubbed DEI content from websites, changed leadership titles, and some renamed themselves entirely. Ohio's Financial Alliance for Racial Equity became Financial Alliance for Representation and Empowerment. The executive orders specifically targeted "large non-profit corporations" and called for enforcement against DEI programs "under whatever name they may appear."

The affected organizations include hospitals, child welfare groups, schools, and community organizations providing critical services. The self-censorship demonstrates the chilling effect of federal threats on civil rights work, as nonprofits prioritize avoiding government scrutiny over their stated missions.

The mass removal of anti-discrimination language from public filings represents institutional capitulation to political pressure against racial equity work. Organizations that previously centered diversity and inclusion in their missions are now hiding or abandoning these commitments to avoid federal retaliation.
