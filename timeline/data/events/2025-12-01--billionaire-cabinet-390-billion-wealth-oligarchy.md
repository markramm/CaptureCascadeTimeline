At least 12 billionaires hold formal Trump administration roles with a combined net worth of approximately $390 billion, making it the wealthiest White House in modern history. The group includes Elon Musk (the world's richest person, who spent $294 million on the 2024 election), the owner of the Houston Rockets, and the former CEO of World Wrestling Entertainment.

For comparison, Biden's cabinet had a combined net worth of $118 million—more than a thousand times less than Trump's billionaire appointees. Even excluding Musk, Trump's 2025 cabinet far exceeds his first term cabinet's $3.2 billion in wealth, which was itself a previous record.

Critics describe this as "a more blatant and transparent form of oligarchy—rule by the super-rich." The pattern demonstrates wealth converting directly to political power and policy control. Billionaires who contributed hundreds of millions to Trump's election now hold positions where they shape policies affecting their business interests.

The concentration of extreme wealth in positions of government power—with individuals whose personal fortunes exceed the GDP of many countries now making regulatory and policy decisions—represents a fundamental transformation of American governance into what observers call "oligarchic rule."
