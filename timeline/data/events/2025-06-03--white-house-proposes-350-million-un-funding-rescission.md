On June 3, 2025, the White House sent a hefty rescission proposal to Congress that would claw back over $350 million in funding from the current and previous fiscal years. The proposal would rescind funding for international peacekeeping missions and would also rescind US funding to the United Nations and other international organizations. The rescission came one day before the US vetoed a UN Security Council ceasefire resolution for Gaza, part of a broader pattern of international isolation and withdrawal from multilateral institutions during the Trump administration.
